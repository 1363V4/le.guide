in a
[previous post](<>),
i (in a regretfully hipster way)
mourned the death of keyboard phones.

who cares. write for contributing to the TALK.
big t

"but no it's the other way around,
no one is steve, in fact we still miss him"...
shut up.

you're steve and you don't get to choose.

rewatch the old keynotes,
he would _constantly_ shit on the competition.
in a way nobody did.
it was not a polite pecker contest
with benchmarks like we have today,
it was rage.

he hated the competition with passion, with precision, with fuel.

why? because **they** started the fight.

he felt personnally offended by the state of things.
he, humble calligraphy enjoyer,
took the ugliness of the world to heart.
and he went on to "fix" it.

there are people who think ai is both
disgusting and inevitable.
a pixel artist: "it's flawed but good enough,
and it's pricing me out".

steve was not a good engineer,
nor a good developer.
and he still went on a crusade on phones and computers.

most importantly: he had a massive fucking ego.
which allowed him to assemble a team.
that's how it is.
human nature.

let me phrase this another way:
if you're depressed and lonely you're fucked.
coincidentally, that's how everything is designed to make you feel these days.
what a world.

so cheer up, phone a friend,
and figure out how to unfuck this mess.
because it is very much **your** problem.
don't look away.

now, we're all steve.

a bit david, too.
